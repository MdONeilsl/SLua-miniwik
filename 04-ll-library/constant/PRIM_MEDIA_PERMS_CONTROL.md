# `PRIM_MEDIA_PERMS_CONTROL`

Integer. This property allows retrieval or modification of the permissions mask that governs who has visibility to the media control bar, utilizing predefined states such as PRIM_MEDIA_PERM_ANYONE, _GROUP, _NONE, or _OWNER.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `14`
