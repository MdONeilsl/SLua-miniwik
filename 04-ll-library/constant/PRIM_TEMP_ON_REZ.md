# `PRIM_TEMP_ON_REZ`

This prim parameter allows retrieval or modification of an object's temporary state. Such transient objects persist until the subsequent garbage collection cycle (approximately one minute) and while they are excluded from standard prim limitations, they are still governed by specific regional constraints.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `4`
