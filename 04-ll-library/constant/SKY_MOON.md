# `SKY_MOON`

Describes various environmental properties related to the moon, including its rotational state, size scaling, luminosity level, default texture status, directional pointing vector, ambient illumination color, and surface scattering color.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `9`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `EnvironmentParamReadOnly`, `EnvironmentParam` |
