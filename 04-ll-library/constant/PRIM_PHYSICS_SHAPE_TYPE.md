# `PRIM_PHYSICS_SHAPE_TYPE`

A property assigned to a prim that allows retrieving or setting the specific geometric shape utilized by the physics engine for that prim (which can be PRIM_PHYSICS_SHAPE_NONE, _PRIM, or _CONVEX). This parameter is primarily leveraged for optimizing physical simulations.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `30`

|---|---|
| `categories` | `PrimParam`, `PrimParamGet` |
