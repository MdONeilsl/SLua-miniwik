# `SKY_REFLECTION_PROBE_AMBIANCE`

This parameter sets the environmental ambient floor level that applies to every reflection probe. Be aware that while it is supported within the GLTF Materials project, its functionality depends on having both compatible viewing environments and proper test setups available.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `24`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `EnvironmentParamReadOnly`, `EnvironmentParam` |
