# `SKY_LIGHT`

This environmental context stores several lighting specifications, including the main light's directional orientation (a unit vector), the immediate light hue (represented by `fade_color` in sRGB coordinates), and the overall background illumination level (the total ambient color, specified in sRGB).

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `8`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `EnvironmentParamReadOnly` |
