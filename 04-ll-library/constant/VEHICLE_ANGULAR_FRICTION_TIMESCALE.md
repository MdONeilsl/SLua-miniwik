# `VEHICLE_ANGULAR_FRICTION_TIMESCALE`

This vector parameter defines the characteristic times (which must be greater than or equal to 0.07 and less than infinity) in seconds for how quickly the angular velocity decays along the three main axes of the vehicle: longitudinal (`at`), lateral (`left`), and vertical (`up`).

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `17`
