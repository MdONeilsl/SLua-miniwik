# `PSYS_PART_FOLLOW_SRC_MASK`

This particle flag ensures that the particles' locations stay linked to both the position and motion of the emitter object. Be aware that activating this flag automatically deactivates the PSYS_SRC_BURST_RADIUS rule.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x10`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `ParticleFlag` |
