# `PRIM_POS_LOCAL`

This parameter, `[PRIM_POS_LOCAL, vector position]`, is used for retrieving or modifying the local coordinates of a primitive (it functions similarly to the `ll.GetLocalPos`/`ll.SetPos` calls).

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `33`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `PrimParam`, `PrimParamGet` |
