# `TERRAIN_PBR_ROTATION_3`

This terrain parameter, when utilized with ll.SetGroundTexture, adjusts the rotational angle of the PBR texture designated for the third layer, measured in radians. Its functionality is restricted solely to PBR textures.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `14`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `GroundTextureParam` |
