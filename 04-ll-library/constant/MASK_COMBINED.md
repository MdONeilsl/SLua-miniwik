# `MASK_COMBINED`

This permission mask setting incorporates an object's inventory items into the calculation of access rights. It is designed to work alongside other existing mask flags, such as MASK_OWNER and MASK_COMBINED.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x10`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `AssetPermissionCategoryFlag` |
