# `SOUND_TRIGGER`

This setting enables sound reproduction by emitting an independent, one-time sound burst at the object's existing position; consequently, it is not bound to or moved by the object itself. Utilizing this flag ensures that its behavior takes precedence over any other available methods for initiating sound playback.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x02`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `PlaySoundFlag` |
