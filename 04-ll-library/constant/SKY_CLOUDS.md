# `SKY_CLOUDS`

This parameter set encapsulates various environmental characteristics of clouds, such as their color, the percentage of sky coverage, scaling factors, degree of randomness (variance), movement speeds along the X and Y axes, parameters controlling cloud density, coefficients for fine details, and whether a default texture is active.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `2`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `EnvironmentParamReadOnly`, `EnvironmentParam` |
