# `PSYS_PART_FOLLOW_VELOCITY_MASK`

This particle flag dictates that the particle should rotate, aligning its designated 'top' (vertical axis) with its vector of motion or creation. If this flag is not active, particles will maintain a fixed vertical orientation, keeping the upper part of their texture facing upward.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x20`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `ParticleFlag` |
