# `VEHICLE_ANGULAR_DEFLECTION_TIMESCALE`

This floating-point parameter dictates the exponential time required for the vehicle to complete its full angular deflection, thereby aligning its desired direction of movement with its actual velocity vector.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `33`
