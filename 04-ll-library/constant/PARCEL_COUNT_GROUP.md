# `PARCEL_COUNT_GROUP`

When utilized alongside ll.GetParcelPrimCount, this constant calculates the cumulative land footprint for items that are either associated with or belong to the parcel's designated group, excluding objects directly owned by the parcel owner.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `2`

|---|---|
| `categories` | `ParcelPrimCountCategory` |
