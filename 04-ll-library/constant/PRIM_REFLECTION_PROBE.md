# `PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE`

This `Prim` parameter serves to configure an object into a custom reflection probe designed specifically for Image-Based Lighting (IBL). The scope of its influence is restricted solely to any geometry positioned inside its designated volume.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `44`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `PrimParam`, `PrimParamGet` |
