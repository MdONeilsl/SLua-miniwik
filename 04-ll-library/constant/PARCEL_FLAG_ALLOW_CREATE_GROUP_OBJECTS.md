# `PARCEL_FLAG_ALLOW_CREATE_GROUP_OBJECTS`

This flag, when utilized with ll.GetParcelFlags, verifies whether individuals who are members of a group or scripts operating within group-owned objects possess permission to generate or recreate objects situated on the parcel.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x4000000`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `ParcelFlag` |
