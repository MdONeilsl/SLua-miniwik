# `ENVIRONMENT_DAYINFO`

Provides details regarding the environment's time and day cycle, including `day_length` (the duration in seconds of one full day cycle), `day_offset` (the shift in seconds relative to GMT), and `secs_since_midnight` (the total seconds that have passed since midnight within the current day cycle).

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `200`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `EnvironmentParamReadOnly` |
