# `PRIM_SPECULAR`

This parameter allows retrieval or modification of the Blinn-Phong specular map texture parameters associated with a face. It mandates the provision of face, texture, repeats, offsets, rotation_in_radians, color, glossy, and environment arguments.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `36`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `PrimParam`, `PrimParamGet` |
