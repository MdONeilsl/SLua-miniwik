# `PSYS_SRC_PATTERN_ANGLE_CONE`

This emission pattern disperses particles radially outward across a three-dimensional volume; this shape can be spherical, conical, or annular, determined by the start/end and inner/outer angle parameters. Setting the angles to 0.0 and PI allows it to replicate the EXPLODE pattern.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x08`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `ParticleSourcePattern` |
