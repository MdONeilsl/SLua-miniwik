# `PRIM_GLTF_NORMAL`

This prim parameter allows retrieval or setting of a GLTF normal map override for a specific face, necessitating the provision of face, texture, repeats, offsets, and rotation arguments.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `45`

| `categories` | `PrimParam`, `PrimParamGet` |
