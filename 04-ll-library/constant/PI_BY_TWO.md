# `PI_BY_TWO`

This value is the mathematical constant $\pi/2$, which corresponds to the measure of radians in a quarter-circle arc. Its utilization within sensor routines denotes that the system should perform a scan limited to one hemisphere.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `1.57079633`

## Deprecated

- **use:** math.pi/2
- **reason:** Double precision.
