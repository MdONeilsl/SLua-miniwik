# `VEHICLE_LINEAR_DEFLECTION_TIMESCALE`

This float parameter, associated with a Vehicle, defines the exponential timescale governing how the vehicle shifts its linear velocity component along its preferred X-axis. Essentially, it quantifies the time required for the vehicle's linear movement to align or redirect towards its designated axis of travel.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `29`
