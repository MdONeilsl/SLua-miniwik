# `PERMISSION_CHANGE_LINKS`

This runtime permission grants scripts the ability to establish, sever, or alter links to other objects through the use of ll.CreateLink, ll.BreakLink, or ll.BreakAllLinks.

**Type:** `number`

**Value:** `0x00080`

## Definition fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `categories` | `ScriptPermission` |
